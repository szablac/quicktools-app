Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-23

This policy describes what cookies QuickTools.hu uses and why. It complements the Privacy Policy.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that your browser stores when you visit a website. Cookies can remember preferences, measure traffic, or personalize advertising.

Cookies we currently use

QuickTools.hu does not currently set any first-party tracking or analytics cookies. The site does not require cookies to function (e.g. to use the tools).

Planned cookies: Google AdSense

We plan to display Google AdSense ads on this site in the future. Once that goes live, Google and its advertising partners may place cookies in your browser:

CategoryPurposeSet by
Ad personalizationMaking displayed ads more relevantGoogle and its advertising partners
MeasurementMeasuring ad performance and trafficGoogle

You can read more in Google's own policies:

  • Google – How Google uses cookies in advertising
  • Google Privacy Policy

Once AdSense goes live, a cookie consent message will appear for visitors from the EEA/UK/Switzerland, where you can accept or decline non-essential cookies.

Managing cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. If you block cookies, some website features (e.g. ad personalization) may not work correctly.

Changes

This policy will evolve alongside the service, especially once AdSense actually goes live.
