Data controller

QuickTools.hu is currently operated by a private individual (a project under active development, not yet a registered business, no final domain name). You can reach the data controller via the contact form at the bottom of this page.

What data we process

The site currently does not offer registration, and does not collect or store personal data from visitors directly. On your first visit, we transiently use your IP address — solely for country lookup, via a locally-run service that never sends any data elsewhere — to decide whether to load the Hungarian or English version of the site; the IP address itself is not stored for this purpose, only the resulting language choice (not the IP address) is saved in a cookie (see the Cookie Policy for details). As the service grows (e.g. user accounts, individual tools), this policy will be updated with the specific data categories and legal bases involved.

Advertising and cookies
